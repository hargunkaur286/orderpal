# Delivery Delays and ETA

*OrderPal is a fictional food-delivery platform. These policies are invented for this project.*

## How the ETA is calculated

The estimated delivery time shown at checkout combines the restaurant's average preparation time for your items, the distance from the restaurant to you, current traffic, and how many delivery partners are free nearby. The ETA can change after the order is placed. The app always shows the latest estimate.

## The on-time promise

Every order has a **promised delivery time**, which is the ETA shown at checkout. If your order arrives late:

- **10 to 30 minutes late:** you receive ₹30 OrderPal Wallet credit automatically.
- **More than 30 minutes late:** the delivery fee is refunded, and you can cancel the order for free if it has not been delivered yet.
- **More than 60 minutes late:** the entire order is refunded, and you can still keep the food if it arrives.

Credits are added automatically within 1 hour of delivery. You do not need to contact support.

## When the promise does not apply

The on-time promise does not apply during:

- Heavy rain or other severe weather, when the app shows a "weather delay" banner before you order.
- Orders where you changed the delivery address after placing the order.
- Large orders above ₹3,000, which may need extra preparation time.

## Tracking

You can track your delivery partner live once the order is **out_for_delivery**. If the map has not moved for more than 10 minutes, contact support from the order page.
