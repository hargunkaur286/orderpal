# Allergens and Food Safety

*OrderPal is a fictional food-delivery platform. These policies are invented for this project.*

## Allergen information on menus

Restaurants on OrderPal tag each menu item with the major allergens it contains: **dairy, gluten, peanut, tree nut, soy, egg, shellfish, and sesame**. These tags are provided by the restaurant. OrderPal checks them during onboarding and every 6 months, but cannot guarantee they are complete.

If you have a severe allergy, add a note in the cooking instructions **and** call the restaurant from the order page before it starts preparing. Cross-contamination in a shared kitchen is always possible, even for items not tagged with an allergen.

## Vegetarian and vegan labels

- A **green dot** means the item is vegetarian: no meat, fish or egg.
- Vegetarian items may contain dairy.
- OrderPal does not currently verify **vegan** or **Jain** claims. Treat those as the restaurant's own description.

## If you have an allergic reaction

Seek medical help first. Then report the order from the app within 24 hours. Allergy reports are escalated to a senior support agent within 1 hour, the restaurant's listing is reviewed, and you receive a full refund for the order.

## Food safety standards

All restaurants must hold a valid food safety licence. Restaurants with a hygiene rating below 3 out of 5 in the last inspection are hidden from search until they are re-inspected. Packaging must be tamper-evident for all orders above ₹500.
