# Refund Policy

*OrderPal is a fictional food-delivery platform. These policies are invented for this project.*

## When you are eligible for a refund

You can request a refund for an order if any of the following happened:

- **Missing items:** one or more items you paid for were not in the bag. You get a refund for the missing items only, not the whole order.
- **Wrong items:** you received an item different from what you ordered. You get a full refund for that item. You do not need to return it.
- **Quality issues:** the food was spilled, stale, or unsafe. Upload a photo within the app. Refunds for quality issues are reviewed by a support agent and capped at the value of the affected items.
- **Order never arrived:** if an order is marked delivered but you did not receive it, report it within 2 hours. After verification you get a full refund including delivery fee and taxes.

## Time limits

Refund requests must be raised **within 48 hours of delivery**. Requests after 48 hours are not accepted, except for orders that never arrived, which must be reported within 2 hours.

## How and when the money comes back

- **OrderPal Wallet:** refunds to the wallet are **instant**.
- **UPI:** 1–3 business days.
- **Credit or debit card:** 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.
- **Cash on delivery orders:** refunded to the OrderPal Wallet only.

You can choose the wallet at the time of the refund to receive money instantly.

## What is not refundable

- Taste preferences ("I didn't like it") when the food was prepared correctly.
- Delivery fee, unless the whole order was not delivered or was delivered more than 30 minutes after the promised time (see the Delivery Delays policy).
- Tips given to the delivery partner. Tips go fully to the partner and cannot be reversed.

## Repeated refund requests

Accounts with more than 5 refund requests in 30 days are reviewed manually. During review, new refund requests may take up to 72 hours to process.
