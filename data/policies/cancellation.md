# Cancellation Policy

*OrderPal is a fictional food-delivery platform. These policies are invented for this project.*

## Free cancellation window

You can cancel any order **free of charge within 60 seconds** of placing it. The full amount is refunded to your original payment method.

## After the restaurant accepts the order

Once the restaurant has accepted the order and started preparing it (status **preparing**), cancelling costs a **cancellation fee equal to 100% of the order value**, because the food is already being made and cannot be resold.

Exceptions where cancellation stays free even after the restaurant accepts:

- The order is **more than 30 minutes past its promised delivery time**.
- The restaurant marks an item as out of stock after you placed the order and you do not accept the substitution.
- The delivery partner cannot be assigned for more than 20 minutes.

## After the order is out for delivery

Orders with status **out_for_delivery** cannot be cancelled from the app. Contact support. If the order is delayed beyond 30 minutes past the promised time, support can cancel it with a full refund.

## Cancellations by the restaurant or OrderPal

If the restaurant or OrderPal cancels your order for any reason, you always get a full refund, including delivery fee, packaging charges and taxes. You also receive a ₹50 OrderPal Wallet credit as an apology, at most once per day.

## Scheduled orders

Scheduled orders can be cancelled free of charge up to 30 minutes before the scheduled time.
